Scoring profile

What counts as a good match. Scores are recalculated on the next fetch — editing these does not rescore postings already in the queue.
One term = weight per line, matched as a substring of the job title. Only the highest-scoring match counts, so a title stuffed with synonyms can't inflate. A posting matching none of these is dropped.
One term = weight per line, matched against the title and description. These stack, but the total is capped at 30 so keyword-stuffed adverts can't dominate.
One term per line. If any appears in the title, the posting is dropped outright. Seniority markers, not topics. Trailing spaces matter — lead avoids matching "leadership".
